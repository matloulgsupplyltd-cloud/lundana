About Lulomine

BUILDING VALUE THROUGH MINERAL RESOURCES

Lulomine is a diamond mining company based in Angola, operating within one of Africa's important mineral-producing regions. Our focus is on developing mining opportunities through responsible operations, technical capability and a long-term commitment to the mining industry.

What we do

OUR MINING OPERATIONS

Lulomine's activities are organised around the diamond mining value chain, from early-stage geological work through to the development of mineral resources. Detailed site information will be published here as it is confirmed and approved for release.

01

Diamond Exploration

Exploration establishes where mineral potential exists and how it can be developed responsibly. It combines geological interpretation, field programmes and technical evaluation before any decision to progress a deposit.

02

Mining and Extraction

Extraction is carried out under planned, supervised and regulated conditions, with attention to worker safety, equipment standards and the control of environmental impact at the working face.

03

Mineral Resource Development

Long-term value depends on disciplined resource management: understanding the deposit, planning its sequenced development and maintaining the technical and regulatory standards that allow operations to continue.

Our presence

PROUDLY ROOTED IN ANGOLA

Diamonds have long been central to Angola's mining sector, with significant diamond-bearing areas in the north-east of the country, including the Lunda provinces. The sector is an important part of Angola's economy and its industrial development.

Lulomine is committed to contributing to Angola's mineral development by operating lawfully, investing in technical capability and engaging constructively with the institutions and communities connected to its work.
