PRIVACY POLICY

This policy explains how Lulomine handles information submitted through this website. It is a general statement and should be reviewed by the company's legal adviser before the site goes live.

Information we collect

When you submit the enquiry form we collect the details you provide: your name, company, email address, telephone number (optional), enquiry type and message. We also record the date and time of the enquiry and your IP address, which are used for security and to prevent abuse.

How we use it

We use this information to read and respond to your enquiry and to keep a record of business correspondence. We do not sell your information or use it for unrelated marketing.

Storage and access

Enquiries are stored on the company's website server and may be forwarded by email to authorised Lulomine staff. Access to stored enquiries is restricted to authorised administrators.

Cookies

The public website does not set tracking cookies. A session cookie is used only when an authorised administrator signs in to manage the site.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by contacting us through the contact page.
